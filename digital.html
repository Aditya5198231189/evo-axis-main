What is Digital Transformation?

Digital Transformation is the process of modernizing and improving every aspect of a business using technology. Instead of relying on disconnected systems and manual work, businesses use AI, Automation, Cloud Platforms, Smart Software and Data Analytics to work faster, reduce costs and deliver better customer experiences.
